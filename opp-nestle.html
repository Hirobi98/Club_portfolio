Nestlé Bangladesh Collaboration

KUET Campus Industry Workshop Partner: Nestlé Bangladesh

About the Opportunity

Spectrum facilitated a collaborative workshop with Nestlé Bangladesh, one of the country's most prominent multinational companies. The session offered KUET students a rare behind-the-scenes look at how one of the world's largest food and beverage corporations operates in the Bangladeshi market.

Nestlé representatives covered key topics including supply chain management from farm to shelf, brand strategy and portfolio management, and the diverse career pathways available to engineering graduates within Nestlé's operations. Students were able to understand what makes multinational FMCG careers both challenging and rewarding.

Session Breakdown

Introduction — Nestlé in Bangladesh

Overview of Nestlé Bangladesh's presence, product lines, and contribution to the local economy. Introduction to the company's talent programs and career development paths.

Deep Dive — Supply Chain & Brand Strategy

Detailed walkthrough of Nestlé's supply chain management processes and how brand strategy is localized for the Bangladeshi market.

Career Panel & Q&A

Open discussion with Nestlé professionals about career journeys, growth opportunities, and how to position yourself for success in FMCG.
