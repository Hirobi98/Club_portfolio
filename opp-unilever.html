Unilever Career Collaboration

KUET Campus Industry Workshop Partner: Unilever

About the Opportunity

Spectrum partnered with Unilever Bangladesh to bring a career-focused workshop directly to KUET students. The session provided hands-on insights into corporate career paths within one of the world's largest consumer goods companies, covering FMCG industry dynamics, talent acquisition processes, and the competencies Unilever looks for in future leaders.

Students had the rare opportunity to engage with Unilever HR professionals and brand managers, gaining first-hand knowledge about what it takes to build a successful career in a multinational FMCG environment. The workshop included interactive exercises, Q&A sessions, and networking with company representatives.

Session Breakdown

Opening — Industry Overview

Unilever representatives introduced the FMCG landscape in Bangladesh, Unilever's local and global footprint, and available career tracks for engineering graduates.

Core Workshop — Career Readiness

Interactive session on professional skills, corporate culture, and what Unilever looks for during recruitment. Covered resume building, assessment centers, and interview techniques.

Closing — Networking & Q&A

Open floor for students to ask questions and connect directly with Unilever HR professionals and brand managers.
