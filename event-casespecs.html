CaseSpecs 3.0

May 03, 2024 KUET Campus Inter-University

About the Event

CaseSpecs 3.0 was Spectrum's flagship inter-university case competition, designed to challenge the brightest minds across Bangladesh with real-world business problems. Teams of 3-4 students developed comprehensive strategies, financial models, and actionable recommendations within a limited timeframe.

The competition tested analytical thinking, teamwork, presentation skills, and the ability to think on one's feet. The third edition raised the bar with more complex cases and an expanded roster of participating universities.

30+ Teams
15+ Universities
50K Prize Pool
3 Rounds

Competition Timeline

Round 1 - Online Screening

Teams submitted initial case analyses online. Top 15 teams were selected for on-campus rounds.

Round 2 - Quarter Finals

Selected teams presented solutions to a panel of industry judges. Evaluated on analytical depth and creativity.

Round 3 - Grand Finale

Top 5 teams competed in a live case challenge, presenting solutions within 3 hours. Winners announced at the closing gala.
