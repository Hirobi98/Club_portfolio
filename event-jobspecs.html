JobSpecs 2025

📅 January 12-13, 2025 📍 KUET Campus 👥 500+ Participants

About the Event

JobSpecs 2025 was KUET's first-ever national job fair, a landmark event organized by Spectrum to bridge the gap between talented engineering graduates and top-tier companies. The two-day event transformed the KUET campus into a bustling recruitment hub, drawing companies from across Bangladesh.

Students had the opportunity to submit resumes, attend on-the-spot interviews, and participate in networking sessions with HR professionals and industry leaders. The event also featured panel discussions on career readiness, professional development workshops, and mock interview sessions.
