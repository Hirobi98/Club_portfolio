KUET Campus Marico Road Show Season 4

About the Event

Marico's flagship "Over the Wall" Season 4 road show came to KUET through Spectrum's partnership with one of South Asia's leading FMCG companies. The road show is Marico's premier campus engagement initiative, designed to connect with the brightest engineering and business talent across Bangladesh.

The KUET edition featured a full day of interactive sessions packed with leadership challenges, innovation sprints, and real-world business case discussions. Participants got a firsthand taste of Marico's dynamic corporate culture and learned directly from company leaders about building careers in FMCG.
